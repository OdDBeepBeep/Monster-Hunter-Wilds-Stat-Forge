## 🌟 Welcome to the Frontier

The **Monster Hunter Wilds Companion Suite** is a thoughtfully engineered utility layer designed for hunters who want to experience every corner of the Forbidden Lands without the usual friction of survival mechanics slowing them down. Rather than acting as a blunt instrument, this suite behaves like a seasoned Palico — quietly at your side, anticipating your needs, and offering only what you ask for.

Whether you are a completionist chasing every endemic life form, a theory-crafter who wants to test wild builds without the grind, or a casual explorer who simply enjoys the scenery of the Wilds, this companion adapts to your rhythm.

The project has grown organically over several release cycles into a robust, feature-rich desktop application with a polished interface, persistent profiles, and compatibility across all three major desktop operating systems.

> Think of it less as a tool, and more as a co-pilot for your hunting journey.

## 🚀 Core Capabilities

The suite is organized around modular "modules" that can be toggled independently. Nothing is forced upon you, and every enhancement is granular. This is not a one-size-fits-all solution — it is a toolbox.

### 🛡️ Vitality & Survivability Enhancements
- Unlimited hunter vitality with smooth regeneration behavior
- Stamina stabilization for extended sprinting and dodging
- Environmental resistance toggles (heat, cold, poison, bleed, blights)
- Underwater oxygen buffer for aquatic encounters
- Fall damage dampening for cliff-heavy regions

### ⚔️ Combat Performance Layer
- Damage amplification sliders with preset intensity tiers
- Critical hit chance adjustment
- Sharpness retention to reduce downtime during hunts
- Wirebug cooldown acceleration
- Focus mode responsiveness tuning

### 🎒 Inventory & Resource Flow
- Renewable item counts for consumables
- Material accumulation modifiers
- Ammo and coating replenishment
- Trap and tool durability extension
- Zenny and research point multipliers

### 🌍 World Interaction Tools
- Time-of-day control for optimal monster spawning
- Weather manipulation for rare endemic life
- Fast travel unlock assistance
- Map marker persistence across sessions
- Scoutfly range extension

### 👥 Squad & Multiplayer Support
- Session stability improvements
- Partner AI tuning where applicable
- Cross-session profile syncing
- Team buff propagation toggles (local session only)

## 🧩 Design Philosophy

Most utilities in this category treat the player as someone who wants to "win". We think that is a limited reading of what gaming can be. The Companion Suite is built on three principles:

1. **Respect for the experience** — Every module is off by default. You opt in deliberately.
2. **Transparency** — Every toggle shows its exact effect. Nothing is hidden behind vague labels.
3. **Longevity** — Updates track official patches so that your configuration carries forward.

This is not about shortcuts. It is about tailoring the sandbox to your taste, the same way you might mod a beloved classic to squeeze out one more playthrough.

## 📚 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this project modify game files on disk?**
No. It operates as a separate process and interacts only with in-memory state during a session.

**Will this interfere with my save data?**
Save integrity is a top priority. Configuration backups are created automatically before any session that alters runtime state.

**Can I use this offline?**
Yes. All modules function without network access. Online features are strictly optional.

**How often is it updated?**
Monthly minor updates, with hotfixes within days of any official patch.

**Is my configuration portable between machines?**
Yes, via the built-in export module. Profiles are simple, human-readable files.

**Do I need programming knowledge?**
Not at all. The interface is point-and-click.
